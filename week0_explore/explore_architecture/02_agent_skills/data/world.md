## Discovered Locations

### Temple of Midgaard
- **Description:** Southern end of temple hall with marble blocks and wall paintings
- **Exits:** n (Temple Altar), e (Donation Room), s (Temple Square), w (Reading Room), d (Temple Square)
- **NPCs:** None
- **Items:** ATM on wall

### Temple Square
- **Description:** Large fountain, marble steps lead to temple
- **Exits:** n (Temple), e (Inn), s (Market Square), w (Clerics' Guild)
- **NPCs:** Fountain, Peacekeeper, Mayor

### Market Square
- **Description:** Famous square, center of Midgaard
- **Exits:** n (Temple Square), e (Main Street east), s (Common Square), w (Main Street west)
- **NPCs:** Mayor

### Main Street (West Section)
- **Description:** Main street with bakery to north, armory to south
- **Exits:** n (Bakery), e (Market Square), s (Armory), w (further west)
- **NPCs:** Peacekeepers

### The Bakery
- **Description:** Sweet scent of danish and fine bread, shelves with items
- **Exits:** s (Main Street)
- **NPCs:** Baker, Peacekeeper
- **Items:** Danish pastry (7g), Bread (14g), Waybread (72g)
