# World Map (Midgaard) — Known Areas

Note: room names repeat ("Main Street") for several distinct rooms — differentiate by description/exits.

## Rooms

### The Weapon Shop
- Desc: metals/oils smell, weaponry everywhere, note on counter.
- NPC: a weaponsmith
- Exits: s -> Main Street (weapon-shop street)

### Main Street (weapon-shop street)
- Desc: weapon shop north, Guild of Swordsmen south, leaves town east, market square west.
- Exits: n -> Weapon Shop, s -> Guild of Swordsmen (entrance hall), w -> Main Street (general-store street), e -> leaves town / East Gate

### Guild of Swordsmen — Entrance Hall
- Exits: n -> Main Street (weapon-shop street)

### Main Street (general-store street)
- Desc: general store north, market place west, Pet Shop south, street continues east.
- Exits: n -> General Store, e -> Main Street (weapon-shop street), w -> Main Street (armory/bakery street), s -> Pet Shop

### The General Store
- Desc: shelves behind counter, note on wall.
- NPC: a grocer
- Exits: s -> Main Street (general-store street)

### Main Street (armory/bakery street)
- Desc: Armory to the south, Bakery to the north, market square to the east.
- Exits: s -> The Armory, n -> The Bakery, e -> Market Square (unconfirmed), w -> Main Street (end-of-street, magic shop area)

### The Armory
- Desc: armor on walls/windows, note on wall.
- NPC: an armorer
- Exits: n -> Main Street (armory/bakery street)
